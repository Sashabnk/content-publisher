Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Content Publisher ("we", "our"), an internal tool used at EuroJET, handles your data.

1. Information We Access

Our application interacts with the TikTok API solely to automate video uploads to our authorized company TikTok account. We specifically request the video.upload and video.publish scopes to transfer locally processed video files directly to the platform. We do not access or collect unrelated personal profile data.

2. Data Usage & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. API access tokens are stored securely on our private server infrastructure and are used exclusively to communicate with official social media platform APIs for publishing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

This service operates using official TikTok APIs. By using this tool, you also comply with TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
